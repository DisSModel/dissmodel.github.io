How the repositories connect

DisSModel spans two GitHub organizations. The packages live in DisSModel; the reference outputs, benchmarks, study cases, workflow and book live in LambdaGeo. Together they form one chain: TerraME and LuccME provide the reference, the goldens and benchmarks check the packages against it, the core runs the models, and the cases, workflow and book show everything working with open data.
